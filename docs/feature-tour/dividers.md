# Dividers

Dividers are **top-level section breaks** in the sidebar — not links. Use them to group menu items visually.

## Create a divider

Use **New divider** in the builder header. Optionally set a short **label** (shown as an uppercase badge on the rule). Leave the label empty for a rule-only break.

## Behaviour

- Dividers always sit at the **top level** — they cannot be nested, and other items cannot nest under them.
- They are inert in the sidebar (no navigation).
- Like manuals, new dividers append at the end of the tree until you drag them into place.
