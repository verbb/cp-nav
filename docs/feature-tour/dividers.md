# Dividers

Dividers visually separate groups of links in the sidebar. For example, you might place a divider labelled `Resources` before links to your team's handbook and support service. A divider has no destination and cannot be clicked to open a page.

<span id="create-a-divider"></span>

## Creating a Divider

Open **Control Panel Nav → Navigation** as an administrator and choose the layout you want to edit. Open the dropdown beside **New menu item** and choose **New divider item**.

Enter `Resources` in **Label** and click **Save** to create the divider. It appears at the end of the menu; drag it above the links you want to group.

Reload the control panel and check that the divider separates the intended links. You can click its label in the builder to edit it again. For a line without text, clear **Label** on the existing divider and save. Leaving the label empty when first creating a divider gives it the default label Divider.

<span id="behaviour"></span>

## Positioning Dividers

Dividers always stay at the top level. They cannot be nested beneath another item, and other items cannot be nested beneath them. Place the links after the divider at the same level to group them visually.
